[ 01 — Ethos ]

One operator. Every discipline. Fifteen years on the water around the world — captaining, repairing, wiring, and caring for vessels with an obsession for the details others overlook.

[ 02 — Who ]

I grew up in a maritime family — the water was never a hobby, it was home. I learned to read the sea before I could drive, and I've spent my life on it ever since.

Over 15 years I've worked as a scientific diver, a rescue diver, and a superyacht captain — from research vessels and remote dive sites to running multi-million-dollar yachts across oceans. I've seen what goes wrong out there, and I know how to keep it from happening.

I'm also a current part-time PhD student specializing in electronics and software — the navigation, networks, and onboard systems most marine crews simply can't touch.

Working independently means leaner pricing and a level of attention to detail a big yard will never give you. Your boat gets treated like it's my own — because to me, every one of them is.
